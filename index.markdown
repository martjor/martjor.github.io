# Welcome!

I am **Jorge A. Martínez-Ortiz**, a PhD student in Computational Mathematics, Science and Engineering at **Michigan State University**. 

My research is focused on the intersection of **Machine Learning** and **Agent-Based Models**, where I develop data-driven simulations to understand and predict the behavior of complex systems. I currently balance my time between my role as a Graduate Research Assistant at MSU and a Graduate Research Intern at **Los Alamos National Laboratory**.

Before moving to Michigan, I completed my Bachelor of Science in Computational Physics at **Baylor University**. I am passionate about bridging the gap between theoretical physics, mathematical modeling, and scalable software solutions.

On this site, you can find my full [Curriculum Vitae](/cv/) and more information [about me](/about/). Thank you for stopping by!
